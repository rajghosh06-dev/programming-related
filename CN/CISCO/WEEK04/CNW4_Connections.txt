The saved file has a complete four-switch mesh: every switch has a direct link

paths; this is expected in a Layer-2 looped topology.

TO UNDERSTAND THE SHAPE:
- Switch0, Switch1, Switch2, and Switch3 form a fully connected mesh.
- Switch3 extends to a daisy-chain section containing Switch4, Switch5, and Switch6.
- Switch6 connects to Switch7, which acts as a star-like access switch for several PCs.
- Switch7 connects onward to Switch8.
- Switch8, Switch9, and Switch10 form a triangular interconnected section.
